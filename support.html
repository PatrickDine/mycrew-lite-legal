Sign-in and access

You need an existing Air Algérie eCrew account authorized for your own use. Lite does not create airline accounts or reset airline passwords. For a locked account, changed password, MFA or portal security challenge, follow the official airline process. A saved report remains readable if a live refresh fails.

The airline permits one active session per account. A deliberate sign-in or reconnect may replace a session used in another app or browser. Lite's background updates never sign in to reclaim it.

Offline reports and downloads

Download the month or range you need before going offline. Only saved coverage is available offline. The displayed successful-download date refers to the saved report; changing the month does not automatically download missing data.

Offline history and appearance

Downloaded months stay saved on this device. Use the month picker’s downloaded-month list to reopen them offline. Zoom and reading position are remembered for each account, month and report range. Auto follows Apple’s appearance; Dark uses black roster paper; Light uses white paper.

Roster changes and flight crew

Yellow columns mark pending eCrew roster changes. Open Roster changes, review the listed dates, and choose Send acknowledgement to confirm them on the official website. An explicit eCrew success response is required; a failed or refused request leaves the pending marker. Day details shows crew already saved with the roster and can download additional details for that duty. Once downloaded, those details are available offline.

Calendar, sharing and printing

Use Share for a PDF, calendar file, CrewLounge PILOTLOG CSV or native print options. The PILOTLOG export uses saved roster times and duration in UTC; review actual flown times and your pilot function before importing through CrewLounge’s Import Wizard. Missing registrations stay blank for the wizard to resolve from the aircraft model. No night, IFR or landing counts are inferred. Calendar permission is optional and needed only for direct calendar updates. If you change calendar providers or devices, review the destination calendar before exporting again. Use Reset Lite calendar export to remove Lite-owned events; unrelated events stay intact.

Devices and data requests

MyCrew Lite supports iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 18 or later. Development installation packages only work on devices covered by their signing profile; they are not public App Store downloads.

For permissions, local removal or service-profile deletion requests, see privacy choices & data removal.

Operational information

Lite is an independent personal roster viewer. Its generated report is not an airline-issued operational document. Always use official company channels for dispatch, check-in, duty changes and flight readiness.
