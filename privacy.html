Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · MyCrew Lite for iPhone and iPad

MyCrew Lite is developed and operated by Mohamed BOUDINE. For privacy questions or data requests, contact mohamed-aero@hotmail.com. This policy covers Lite, not every feature of the separate MyCrew Roster app.

Information used by the app

  • eCrew access: your crew ID, password and session information are used to sign in to Air Algérie's eCrew portal. Saved credentials and session cookies use Lite's own device-only Keychain entries. The roster normalization request does not include your password, cookies or airline session token.
  • Roster information: the app receives your published duties, flights, dates, routes, crew profile and available supporting report details. Depending on what eCrew supplies, this can include colleague assignments, contacts, hotels, training or qualification expiry information.
  • Local information: Lite stores downloaded roster data, report files, download dates, account-scoped preferences and identifiers for its exported calendar events.

How information moves

During sign-in or an authorized update, your device communicates with Air Algérie eCrew. The device sends your crew ID, selected month, source schedule events and dashboard content to MyCrew's existing Supabase service to normalize the report. As verified on 11 November 2026, current Lite requests do not create or update a shared crew profile because they do not include a MyCrew owner-session token. Earlier service versions could retain a basic profile: crew ID, display name, rank, base, fleet, crew group and verification date. Existing profiles may remain in shared MyCrew infrastructure. Lite does not expose Marketplace or social features.

Air Algérie operates the source account and portal. Supabase provides backend processing and storage. These providers process information needed to operate their services, including request metadata such as IP addresses and security or error logs. Infrastructure logging and retained shared profiles mean this service is not described as entirely on-device or as collecting no data.

When the network is used

Opening a compatible saved report, browsing saved months and changing display settings work locally. Live roster access happens for sign-in, an explicit download, refresh, reconnect or credential update, explicitly requested crew details or roster acknowledgement, or a background opportunity you separately enable. Background updates are off by default and do not sign in or force session takeover. Opening an external policy, support or eCrew link uses your browser and its network connection.

Optional Apple features and sharing

Calendar export requests permission only when you use it. Lite reads writable calendar information and checks its own event markers to update or remove the events it created. It does not upload your personal calendar to the roster service or edit unrelated events. A calendar account provider may sync exported events under your own calendar settings.

Change notifications are local and optional. Sharing, printing, PDF and calendar-file exports send information only to the destination you choose; those copies remain under your control. Lite has no dedicated iCloud synchronization. Device backups may include app files and preferences under your Apple settings; Lite's saved credentials use the Keychain's ThisDeviceOnly protection.

No advertising or tracking

Lite contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK or cross-app tracking. It does not request GPS, device contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. Flight airports come from your roster, not physical location tracking. We do not sell personal information.

Retention, removal and rights

Saved report history stays in Lite's private app storage until removed. Signing out removes Lite's saved credentials and session entries but deliberately retains offline roster history. Calendar events and files exported elsewhere are not removed by signing out or deleting the app. See data choices for removal instructions.

The shared service profile remains until it is updated or removed; signing out does not delete it. You can request access, correction or deletion by email. We verify the requester's identity without asking for an eCrew password and explain any effects on shared MyCrew services. Service logs, support correspondence and backups may be retained for service operation, security, backup rotation or legal obligations. This policy does not promise a fixed provider log or backup deletion period.

You may have additional rights under applicable privacy law, including the right to raise a concern with a relevant authority. Contact mohamed-aero@hotmail.com to exercise your rights. Airline-held employment and eCrew account records must be managed with Air Algérie.

Security, audience and updates

Lite uses HTTPS, iOS application isolation and the Keychain for saved access. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and do not share your account or report files with unauthorized people. This professional crew tool is not directed to children.

When practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated. This website does not add advertising or analytics code. GitHub Pages hosts these public support and policy pages. GitHub processes ordinary request metadata, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its service under the GitHub privacy statement. Hosting practices are separate from Lite’s native application.

Optional crew details, acknowledgements and exports

When you request flight crew details, Lite reads that duty directly from the airline and saves the supplied information privately on your device for offline viewing. Missing contacts are not invented. When you explicitly send a roster acknowledgement, the pending change identifiers are sent to eCrew and the confirmation is stored locally. Viewing or downloading a roster does not acknowledge it. Reading positions, zoom and appearance preferences stay on the device. CrewLounge PILOTLOG CSV files are created locally from the selected saved roster; Lite does not upload them to CrewLounge. You choose any recipient through Apple’s sharing interface.
