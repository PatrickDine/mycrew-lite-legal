Purpose and authorized use

MyCrew Lite is an independent personal roster viewer for crew with an existing, authorized Air Algérie eCrew account. Use only an account and information you are permitted to access. Follow your employer's rules and protect your credentials and colleagues' personal information. Lite does not create an airline account, grant employment access or authorize use of an employer's data.

Saved reports can be out of date. Generated reports, calendar entries, notifications and exports are provided for personal organization and do not replace official dispatch documents, check-in procedures or company notices. Verify operational requirements through official airline channels.

Live functions depend on eCrew, the existing MyCrew processing service, connectivity and your account permissions. Access can be interrupted or changed. The airline's single-session rules can cause a deliberate login or reconnect to replace another app or browser session. A successful calendar export or notification does not prove a duty has been acknowledged or accepted by the airline.
