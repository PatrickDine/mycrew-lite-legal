Your data choices

Manage MyCrew Lite without giving anyone your eCrew password.

Control optional access

  • In Lite's Settings → Background & notifications, turn off background roster updates and roster-change notifications.
  • In Apple's Settings, revoke MyCrew Lite's Calendar or notification permission whenever you choose.
  • Reading an already saved report does not require a fresh airline login.

Remove exported calendar events

In Lite's Settings → Calendar settings, choose Reset Lite calendar export while the relevant account is signed in and calendar access is available. This removes the events Lite recorded as its own. It preserves unrelated calendar entries. Copies imported manually from an ICS file may need to be removed in your calendar app.

Remove local app data

  1. Remove any Lite-created calendar events you no longer want, if applicable.
  2. Use Sign out in Lite to clear saved credentials and session entries. Signing out alone retains private roster history.
  3. To remove Lite's remaining local report history and preferences, choose Delete App in iPhone/iPad Settings → General → Storage → MyCrew Lite. Offload App retains app data and is not the same as deletion.
  4. Remove any PDF/ICS copies you exported elsewhere and review your device backups separately.

Keychain entries can survive app removal under Apple's system behavior; sign out before deleting the app to clear Lite's saved access. None of these steps deletes your employer-issued eCrew account or signs other applications out.

Request removal of a service profile

The existing roster service can save a basic profile associated with your crew ID. To request access, correction or deletion, email mohamed-aero@hotmail.com with the subject MyCrew Lite data request. Include your crew ID and what you want removed. Do not include a password, cookie, token or an unredacted roster.

We will verify ownership, explain any effect on the separate MyCrew Roster service, and handle the request subject to applicable law and legitimate security or retention requirements. The airline controls deletion or correction of its own source records. This contact route is not a claim that the app has an automated backend-account deletion feature.
